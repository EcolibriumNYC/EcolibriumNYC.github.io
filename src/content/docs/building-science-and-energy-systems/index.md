## Notes

### Buildings are systems

- Building science is complex. It's the interaction between:
  - the **building** itself
  - the **local climate**
  - the **occupants**
  - the **HVAC, energy systems, and other appliances**
- Changing one affects the others.

### Tight envelope vs. ventilation

- Making the building envelope tight saves energy: less heated or cooled air leaks out.
- But people need fresh air. A tight building needs ventilation for good indoor air quality.
- Balancing these two is a central tension in building design.

### Reducing stress on the grid

We approach it in three steps:

1. **Make buildings more efficient**, so they need less energy in the first place.
2. **Electrify** (for example, heat pumps instead of gas) to meet carbon goals and find new efficiencies.
3. **Offset with new energy assets at the edge**, including solar (PV) and batteries.

### How this shapes our work

- These tradeoffs are what we design our smart home and VPP system around at Loisaida.
