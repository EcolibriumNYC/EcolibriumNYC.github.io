This site helps students, interns, hackers, and tinkerers learn what they need to contribute to EcolibriumNYC's projects.

New here? Start with [Getting Started](/getting-started/).

## Projects

- **Virtual Power Plant (VPP):** a zero-cloud virtual power plant that helps communities become resilient energy prosumers.
- **LES Solar Map:** a map estimating building solar potential across NYC's Lower East Side.
- **Thermal Camera:** a portable handheld thermal camera for building envelope audits and thermographic studies.
